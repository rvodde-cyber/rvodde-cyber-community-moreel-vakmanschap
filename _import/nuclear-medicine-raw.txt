Between Compressions

The compressions don't stop for anyone, not even for a blood draw. Tomas kneels beside the gurney, waiting for the half-second window between the chest going down and the chest coming back up. He needs a potassium level — clean, uncontaminated, no hemolysis — because that number might decide whether the team keeps going or calls it. Draw too slow, apply too much stasis, and the result lies: it says the heart can't be saved when maybe it still could be.
